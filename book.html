About the Book

Evolve or Die: The Architecture of Cooperation explores the critical interplay between technical architecture, organisational economics, and governance frameworks. Covering monolith-to-microservice tradeoffs, decentralized and AI-driven architectures, and a unified cooperation framework — Goals, Values, and Guardrails — this book bridges executive strategy with engineering reality.

Deep examination of data governance, cloud-native patterns, and the economic forces shaping modern enterprise technology — from C-suite vision to production execution.
